## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a tranquil, sovereign, and human-centric visual language for personal identity reconciliation. Handling primary Indian identity credentials—such as Aadhaar, PAN, Passports, and Driving Licences—demands an atmosphere of supreme institutional competence without bureaucratic friction or intimidation.

The aesthetic synthesizes modern consumer fintech restraint with high-integrity data clarity:
- **Personality:** Stoic, protective, precise, and empowering. It removes anxiety from discrepancies like name transliterations, expanded initials, and mismatched dates of birth.
- **Design Movement:** Modern Architectural Minimalism paired with soft tactile surfaces. It prioritizes crisp hairline dividers, deep ink-toned typographic hierarchy, generous rhythmic whitespace, and micro-diffused ambient depth over heavy dropshadows or sensory noise.
- **Trust Metaphor:** Non-governmental and citizen-first. All surfaces reinforce user ownership through soft security badges, crystalline review containers, and transparent comparison structures.

## Colors

The palette establishes an immediate sensation of optical clarity, emotional calm, and disciplined hierarchy. It avoids pure black and harsh alerts in favor of calibrated slate and jewel-toned state signifiers.

### Canvas & Surfaces
- **Canvas Base:** `#F8FAFC` (Slate 50) serves as the warm, eye-relieving foundation for full screens.
- **Card Surface:** `#FFFFFF` creates crisp elevation contrast against the slate canvas.
- **Muted Surface:** `#F1F5F9` (Slate 100) handles nested wells, input backgrounds, and inactive comparisons.

### Primary Ink & Structural Accents
- **Primary Ink / Foundation:** `#0F172A` (Slate 900) for structural framing, core branding, high-priority headings, and primary buttons.
- **Secondary Ink / Slate:** `#1E293B` (Slate 800) for secondary headers and document data strings.
- **Electric Action Accent:** `#2563EB` (Blue 600) for interactive anchors, active tab markers, progress paths, and primary focus rings.

### State & Document Consistency Signals
- **Verified Match:** `#10B981` (Emerald 500) paired with `#ECFDF5` (Emerald 50). Used strictly when documents share verified identical parity.
- **Advisory / Review Needed:** `#F59E0B` (Amber 500) paired with `#FFFBEB` (Amber 50). Applied to non-blocking differences such as expanded initials, honorific variations, or differing address syntax.
- **Blocking Conflict:** `#EF4444` (Rose 500) paired with `#FEF2F2` (Rose 50). Strictly reserved for critical failures like disparate dates of birth or fundamentally conflicting names across PAN and Aadhaar.
- **Neutral Outlines & Dividers:** Hairline `#E2E8F0` (Slate 200) at 80%–100% opacity for precise document compartmentalization.

## Typography

The type system blends the human, contemporary proportions of **Plus Jakarta Sans** with the structural, tabular rigor of **Inter**.

- **Headlines & Narrative (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Generous x-height, clear geometry, and warm terminals remove institutional coldness while preserving authority. Negative tracking on larger headings (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) creates a polished editorial appearance.
- **Labels, System Microcopy & Document Records (Inter):** Applied across field descriptors, tabular comparisons, masked identification numbers (e.g., `XXXX-XXXX-1234`), and verification timestamps. Inter ensures unambiguous character distinction between `0` and `O`, `1` and `l`.
- **Numeric Clarity:** Ensure `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` is active across all document values, validity timelines, and percentage match counters to avoid layout jank during dynamic status evaluations.

## Layout & Spacing

A strictly bounded responsive fluid grid prevents critical identity comparisons from fragmenting or visually disassociating across extreme monitor widths.

### Grid Anatomy
- **Desktop (>= 1024px):** Max container width of `1120px` to maintain focused inspection bounds. 12-column layout with `1.5rem` gutters and dynamic auto-centering canvas margins. Comparison side-by-sides (e.g., PAN vs. Aadhaar) map cleanly into dual 6-column panels.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column layout, `1.5rem` gutters, and `1.5rem` outer margins.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout, `1rem` gutters, and `1rem` outer margins. Side-by-side verification rows transform into unified stacked comparison tokens.

### Spacing Philosophy
Space dictates trust:
- High-density data clusters (document number, issued name, father's name) utilize internal `space-xs` and `space-sm` gaps for grouping logic.
- Card padding remains expansive (`space-lg` to `space-xl`) to establish an uncluttered, premium reading posture.

## Elevation & Depth

To avoid the artificial appearance of floating components and project institutional stability, depth relies on low-contrast structural outlines paired with subtle, ambient daylight shadows.

### Depth Hierarchy
1. **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** `#F8FAFC`, flat. Holds background navigation and static breadcrumbs.
2. **Level 1 (Card & Modular Wells):** `#FFFFFF` surface enclosed by a hairline border: `1px solid rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.8)`. Shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`.
3. **Level 2 (Active Inspection / Hovered Card):** Border transitions to `rgba(203, 213, 225, 0.9)`. Ambient shadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`.
4. **Level 3 (Modals, Overlays, and Drawer Panels):** Background `#FFFFFF` with boundary `1px solid #E2E8F0`. Shadow: `0 20px 32px -8px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`. Backdrops use `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.4)` with an optical blur of `4px`.

## Shapes

The geometric framework uses calibrated curves that communicate welcoming consumer friendliness without sacrificing systemic precision.

- **Standard Cards & Document Panels:** Standardized on `rounded-2xl` (`1rem` / `16px`) for outer boundaries to provide a friendly, reassuring container for sensitive documents.
- **Internal Wells, Input Fields, and Nested Nodes:** Standardized on `rounded-xl` (`0.75rem` / `12px`) to maintain harmonious concentric rhythm within parent cards.
- **Chips, Badges, and Micro-Indicators:** Full pill curves (`rounded-full`) to delineate metadata tags from clickable rectilinear elements.
- **Buttons:** Modern rounded rectangles (`rounded-xl` / `12px`), matching interactive form controls to project structural cohesion.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#0F172A`, text `#FFFFFF`, radius `rounded-xl`. High-contrast focus state with a `2px` ring in `#2563EB` with `2px` offset. Hover shifts gracefully to `#1E293B`.
- **Secondary:** Background `#FFFFFF`, text `#0F172A`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`. Hover triggers background `#F8FAFC` and border `#CBD5E1`.
- **Tertiary / Subtle:** Flat `#F1F5F9`, text `#334155`. Hover shifts to `#E2E8F0`.

### Chips & Trust Badges
- **Non-Governmental Trust Signifiers:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), padded with `0.25rem 0.75rem`. Features an SVG lock or shield icon. Uses background `#F8FAFC` bordered with `#E2E8F0` and `#475569` text (e.g., "Private & Secure", "User-Controlled Consistency").
- **Match Status Chips:**
  - *Identical:* Background `#ECFDF5`, text `#065F46`, hairline border `1px solid #A7F3D0`.
  - *Review Needed:* Background `#FFFBEB`, text `#92400E`, hairline border `1px solid #FDE68A`.
  - *Conflict:* Background `#FEF2F2`, text `#991B1B`, hairline border `1px solid #FECACA`.

### Cards & Document Inspection Containers
- Constructed on `#FFFFFF` using `rounded-2xl` and hairline `#E2E8F0` borders.
- Header bands integrate clean metadata slots: Indian document type moniker, truncated credential sequence (`•••• 8821`), and an explicit parity badge.
- Comparative views present values side-by-side with an inline difference highlighter highlighting string variances (e.g., "VIKRAM S. MEHTA" vs "VIKRAM SURESH MEHTA").

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Inputs:** Height `2.75rem`, background `#FFFFFF`, text `#0F172A`, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`, corner radius `rounded-xl`. Focus states feature crisp electric blue focus rings (`#2563EB`). Helper text utilizes `body-sm` in `#64748B`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Curved checks (`rounded-md`) and radios (`rounded-full`) in primary navy `#0F172A` when checked, bordered with `#94A3B8` when unselected.

### Lists & Comparison Rows
- Comparison key-value lists use alternated or divider-delimited lines with subtle `1px solid #F1F5F9` separators. Labels use `label-sm` in uppercase slate, and values use bold, tabular-num typography to promote fast visual scanning.
